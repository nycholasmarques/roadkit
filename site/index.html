Everything a study plan needs

Each roadmap is a JSON file. Roadkit turns it into a place where the work happens.

🗺️

Map and kanban

The same roadmap as a path or a board. Each topic moves through to do, in progress, in review and finished, with tasks and sub-topics.

☀️

A Today screen

What is in progress across every roadmap and the next step of each one, on a single screen.

🛠️

Projects by level

Hands-on projects from beginner to advanced, linked to the topics they practice, with requirements to check off.

📝

Markdown notes

A notebook on every topic and project: the command that worked, the open question, the decision you made.

📈

Statistics

A heatmap of your year, your day streak and a forecast of when each roadmap ends at your pace.

🔁

Review and focus

Optional: finished topics come back for spaced review, and a focus timer records the real time each topic took.

⌨️

Search and shortcuts

Ctrl+K goes to any topic of any roadmap, and single-letter keys do the rest, with the key shown on each button.

📲

App, offline, shareable

Install it as an app, use it with no internet, and export a single page to show your progress to anyone.

🤖

Create with AI

Describe what you want to learn, take the prompt to any AI you like, and the roadmap opens in the editor. No API key.

A look around

The real app, with the example roadmaps and a few months of use.

Start the day on Today

Everything in progress across your roadmaps, the most recently touched first, the reviews due and the next topic of each one. Press H from anywhere.

Projects that unlock as you learn

Each project lists the topics it uses. Finish them and it is ready to start; check off its requirements, light up the 🔥 extra challenges and keep the link to your repository.

See your pace, not a scoreboard

A heatmap of your year, your day streak and, for each roadmap, the weekly pace and when it should end at that pace. No warnings when you take a break.

Notes that stay with the topic

Markdown on every topic and project: the command that worked, the question, the decision. Search finds them, and they come out together as Markdown.

Seven themes, light and dark

Light, Mist and Paper; Night, Graphite, Plum and Black. Pick one for each mode and let it follow your system.

Made for the phone too

The map becomes a timeline, the panels open as drawers, and installed as an app it works with no internet.

See it move

Check off a topic, drag a card on the kanban, switch to dark. Or skip the video and open the real app with the example roadmaps: nothing to install, and your progress stays in your browser.

Why it is different

  • Free and open, for good. MIT licensed. Nothing paid, no account, no telemetry.
  • Your data is files you understand. Roadmaps and progress are readable JSON, in a folder you can back up.
  • Runs on your machine. Python with no dependencies, or a small Docker image. No build step.
  • Works offline and on your phone. Install it as an app; changes made offline sync when you are back.
  • Portuguese and English. The interface and the docs come in both, and roadmaps can be in any language.

Install

With Docker, one command. Then open http://localhost:8765.

docker run -d -p 127.0.0.1:8765:8765 -v roadkit-data:/app/data ghcr.io/nycholasmarques/roadkit:1

Or with nothing but Python 3: download the latest release, unzip it and run:

python3 tools/roadkit.py serve
